Google Cloud

Google for Startups Cloud Program

$2,000 no investor / $200,000 Scale / $350,000 AI

No referral needed easy to get Cloud

Who qualifies

  • Start tier: explicitly for startups not yet backed by equity funding - up to $2,000
  • Scale tier: requires startup equity funding - up to $100,000 in year one plus up to $100,000 in year two
  • AI startups: up to $350,000 in cloud cost coverage
  • Founded within 5 years of applying, or 10 years for Scale
  • Google Cloud account with a Billing ID, a public company website, and email on that same domain
