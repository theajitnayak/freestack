Who qualifies

  • Actively developing a technology product

What to know

The best-documented programme on this list. Tier 3 is explicitly for bootstrapped or self-funded startups with under $1M raised and needs no partner at all - you can apply directly for $10,000. Tiers 1 and 2 require funding from an affiliated partner within the last 12 months.
